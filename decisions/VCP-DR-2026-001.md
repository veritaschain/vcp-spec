## 4. Rationale

No route from proposal to adoption is defined for VCP. Proposals merged as discussion documents, such as the 2026-10-24 integrity drafts, therefore cannot become adopted, however long they stay open.

VAP §10.3 assigns decisions to a Technical Committee vote, but no Technical Committee membership or decision record for VCP has been published. This procedure makes a single-editor decision after public review explicit and checkable. It discloses that it is not an independent consensus process, and it ends when a Technical Committee meeting the criteria in §10 is constituted.

**Bootstrap limitation.** The procedure is adopted by the same editor it empowers. The 30-day public review and this record are the only safeguards available for this first decision, and that limitation is recorded here rather than concealed.
